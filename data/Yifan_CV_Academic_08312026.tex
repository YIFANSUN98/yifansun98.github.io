\section{RESEARCH STATEMENT}

My research develops intelligent decision-making and motion-control methods that enable complex robotic systems---especially humanoids---to operate safely in dynamic, contact-rich environments. I study scalable safety analysis, safe learning and control, and dexterous whole-body interaction, with applications to human--robot collaboration and industrial automation.
